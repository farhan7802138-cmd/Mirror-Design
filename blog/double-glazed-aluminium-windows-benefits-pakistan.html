Double-Glazed Aluminium Windows: Benefits for Noise and Heat in Pakistan

R
Master Rahman
Master Fabricator • Rahman Aluminium & Glass Works Sialkot

As electricity tariffs soar across Pakistan and urbanization increases street traffic and noise pollution in vibrant cities like Sialkot, Gujranwala, and Lahore, the architectural specification of windows has shifted from a mere decorative detail into a critical functional component of home construction. Traditional single-pane glass windows encased in lightweight aluminium or unseasoned wood are increasingly seen as architectural liabilities. Modern homeowners are upgrading to acoustic double-glazed aluminium window systems for tangible savings on air conditioning and peaceful indoor living.

How Double-Glazed Insulated Glass Units (IGUs) Work

A double-glazed window consists of two separate panes of tempered glass (typically 5mm or 6mm thick each) separated by a hermetically sealed aluminum spacer bar filled with moisture-absorbing desiccant crystals. The sealed cavity between the two panes—usually 9mm, 12mm, or 16mm in width—is either vacuum-sealed or filled with inert, non-toxic argon gas. This creates a powerful thermal and acoustic buffer that conduction cannot easily cross.

1. Dramatic Heat Resistance and Air Conditioning Savings

In Punjab, outdoor daytime temperatures routinely surpass 42°C throughout May, June, and July. Single-pane glass acts like an open thermal radiator, absorbing exterior heat and conducting it straight into your living room. Your air conditioner runs continuously, consuming peak-rate electricity units while struggling to bring room temperatures down.

Double-glazed windows boast an exceptional U-value (a measure of heat transfer rate) compared to single glazing:

  • Single Glaze 5mm Glass: U-value of approx. 5.8 W/m²K (allows massive heat transfer).
  • Standard Double Glaze (5mm + 12A + 5mm): U-value drops to approx. 2.8 W/m²K—reducing heat transfer by over 50%.
  • Double Glaze with Low-E Reflective Coating: U-value plummets below 1.8 W/m²K, reflecting up to 70% of infrared solar radiation while letting pleasant visible light illuminate the interior.

For a typical 10-marla or 1-kanal house in Sialkot Cantt or Model Town, this thermal performance translates into a 20% to 35% reduction in monthly summer air conditioning electricity consumption.

2. Acoustic Soundproofing Against Road Traffic and Industrial Noise

Whether your property is located near the commercial bustle of Kashmir Road, adjacent to a manufacturing facility in Ugoki, or facing highway transit on Wazirabad Road, persistent external noise degrades sleep quality and mental wellness. Sound waves travel easily through thin, vibrating single glass panes.

Double-glazed acoustic windows dismantle sound energy through dual-density acoustic attenuation. When sound waves hit the first pane of glass, they lose energy; the air gap disrupts the wave frequency; and the second pane further dampens the vibration. By utilizing asymmetrical glass thicknesses (for example, pairing a 6mm outer pane with a 4mm inner pane), sound wave resonance is eliminated, reducing incoming ambient street noise by up to 35 to 38 decibels. Heavy truck rumbles, motorcycle exhausts, and loud generator hums are reduced to a faint, easily ignored whisper.

3. Complete Elimination of Winter Condensation and Mildew

During cold Punjab winters when indoor heaters or geysers operate, warm humid indoor air hits freezing single-pane window glass, causing water droplets to stream down the glass onto sills, walls, and curtains. Over time, this condensation rots plaster, damages paint, and fosters harmful black mold spores. In a double-glazed window, the inner pane remains close to room temperature because it is thermally decoupled from the cold exterior pane, completely eliminating interior condensation.

4. Heavy-Gauge Aluminium Profile Engineering

An insulated glass unit is heavy—often weighing 25 to 35 kg per window sash. Installing heavy double glazing into flimsy, lightweight commercial aluminium sections results in bowed frames, warped tracks, and broken rollers. At Rahman Aluminium & Glass Works, we exclusively build our double-glazed windows using heavy-gauge architectural aluminium profiles (1.6mm to 2.0mm structural wall thickness), reinforced steel-bearing dual nylon rollers, and multi-point perimeter compression seals that ensure effortless sliding and lifelong structural integrity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can existing single-glazed aluminium windows be converted to double-glazed?
If your existing aluminium frame section is deep enough (typically requiring a 75mm to 100mm track profile), we can replace the single glass bead with a specialized double-glazing bead. However, if the existing frame is a lightweight section, upgrading to a complete heavy-gauge frame is recommended.
Will the air space inside double-glazed windows become dusty or cloudy over time?
No. Factory-assembled insulated glass units are hermetically sealed with double-barrier primary polyisobutylene and secondary structural silicone, with internal molecular sieve desiccants that absorb any microscopic moisture. If a seal ever fails, it is replaced under warranty.
How much more expensive is double glazing compared to single glazing?
Double glazing typically increases window fabrication cost by approximately 30% to 45% compared to high-end single glazing. However, the energy savings from lower air conditioning and heating bills typically pay back the initial difference within 3 to 4 years.
Does double-glazed glass provide improved home security?
Significantly so. An intruder must break through two separate sheets of tempered glass rather than one, creating double the noise and physical barrier, which deters unauthorized entry.
