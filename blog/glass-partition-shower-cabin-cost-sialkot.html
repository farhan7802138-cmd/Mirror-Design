How Much Does a Glass Partition or Shower Cabin Cost in Sialkot?

R
Master Rahman
Master Fabricator • Rahman Aluminium & Glass Works Sialkot

From sleek corporate executive cabins along Sialkot’s export corridors to luxurious master bathroom walk-in showers in Model Town and Cantt villas, architectural glass has become the hallmark of upscale modern interior architecture in Pakistan. But when budgeting for a commercial office divider or residential glass shower cabin, many clients find pricing estimates difficult to decipher. Understanding square-footage rates, glass tempering grades, and stainless steel hardware specifications ensures you get high safety and durability without paying inflated retail markups.

Glass Thickness Standards: 8mm, 10mm, and 12mm Tempered

Under Pakistani building codes and international safety standards, raw annealed float glass must never be used in frameless interior partitions or shower enclosures. When broken, standard glass shatters into dagger-like shards that cause grievous injuries. All professional installations by Rahman Aluminium & Glass Works exclusively utilize Grade-A safety tempered glass, which is thermally toughened to five times the strength of regular glass and crumbles into small, harmless granular pebbles if fractured under extreme force.

Pricing is fundamentally anchored in glass thickness and square footage:

  • 8mm Tempered Glass: Best suited for compact residential shower screens, framed sliding cubicles, and lightweight bathroom divider panels. Lighter weight means less strain on hinges. Approximate glass-only fabrication cost ranges from Rs. 420 to Rs. 550 per square foot.
  • 10mm Tempered Glass (The Gold Standard): The ideal specification for residential frameless shower cabins, walk-in glass shower screens, and standard commercial office dividers up to 9 feet tall. It offers outstanding structural rigidity without excessive deadweight. Approximate glass-only fabrication cost ranges from Rs. 580 to Rs. 750 per square foot.
  • 12mm Tempered Glass: Mandatory for floor-to-ceiling office partitions exceeding 9.5 feet in height, frameless pivot glass doors, commercial storefronts, and high-traffic shopping plaza entries. Delivers supreme structural stability and acoustic dampening. Approximate glass-only fabrication cost ranges from Rs. 800 to Rs. 1,050 per square foot.

Shower Cabin Cost Breakdown: Hardware Quality Matters

In a bathroom shower enclosure, the glass accounts for roughly 45% of the total budget—the remaining 55% is determined by hardware fittings, waterproofing gaskets, and installation labor. Hardware must endure continuous exposure to hard water, soaps, and steam:

  • Grade 304 Stainless Steel vs. Grade 201: Beware of cheap contractor packages that use Grade 201 stainless steel or chrome-plated zinc alloys. In Sialkot’s water conditions, low-grade fittings develop unsightly rust spots within six months. At Rahman Aluminium & Glass Works, we exclusively install certified Grade 304 anti-rust stainless steel glass brackets, heavy-duty 90°/180° wall hinges, overhead stabilizer bars, and magnetic water-seal strips.
  • Walk-In Fixed Glass Screen (Single Panel, 3ft x 7ft): Rs. 18,000 – Rs. 28,000 fully installed with Grade 304 floor channels, wall clamps, and top stabilizer arm.
  • Corner L-Shape Frameless Shower Cabin (3ft x 3ft or 4ft x 4ft with Swing Door): Rs. 42,000 – Rs. 68,000 fully installed with heavy pivot hinges, magnetic seals, transparent bottom sweep, and stainless towel-bar handle.
  • Sliding Roller Frameless Shower Enclosure: Rs. 48,000 – Rs. 75,000 featuring heavy-duty top exposed stainless steel sliding bar rollers for smooth, noiseless glides in tighter bathroom layouts.

Commercial Office Glass Partitions: Square Foot Estimates

For executive suites, conference boardrooms, and medical clinics in Sialkot, a complete turnkey office partition system (including 10mm or 12mm tempered glass, slim anodized aluminium perimeter tracks, floor springs, patch fittings, and overhead locks) typically ranges from Rs. 950 to Rs. 1,450 per square foot of installed wall area.

If you require custom privacy, we offer several finishes:

  • Plotter-Cut Frosted Privacy Film: Rs. 60 to Rs. 120 per sq. ft. for clean geometric bands, company logos, or gradient privacy transitions.
  • Permanent Acid Etching or Sandblasting: Rs. 150 to Rs. 250 per sq. ft. for durable, scratch-proof matte privacy that never peels.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to fabricate and install a shower cabin in Sialkot?
Once exact laser measurements are verified, factory glass cutting, edge polishing, tempering, and hardware preparation takes 5 to 7 working days. On-site installation is completed cleanly within 4 to 6 hours.
Does tempered glass scratch or stain easily in hard water?
Tempered glass has a very dense surface that resists light scratches. To prevent mineral stains from Sialkot hard groundwater, we apply a hydrophobic nano-coating that repels water droplets and makes cleaning effortless with a simple squeegee.
Can glass office partitions block boardroom conversation noise?
Yes. 10mm tempered glass delivers an acoustic rating around 33 dB, while 12mm acoustic laminated glass achieves 37–39 dB. Combined with acoustic perimeter silicone seals, it provides complete executive confidentiality.
Do you provide on-site measurement before cutting the glass?
Always. Tempered glass cannot be cut, drilled, or trimmed once it has completed the thermal oven tempering cycle. Our technicians perform precision on-site laser surveys before manufacturing commences.
