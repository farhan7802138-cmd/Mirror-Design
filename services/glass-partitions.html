WHY CHOOSE OUR COMMERCIAL & CORPORATE GLASS

Modern workspaces in Sialkot’s corporate, export, and industrial sectors demand open, light-filled environments without sacrificing speech confidentiality. Rahman Aluminium & Glass Works specializes in floor-to-ceiling 10mm and 12mm heavy tempered glass partitions. Built with minimal structural perimeter aluminium tracks or sleek black framing, our glass partition walls provide exceptional acoustic attenuation (38–42 dB) and can be custom frosted with privacy bands or company logos. Installation takes only 24 to 48 hours with zero drywall dust.

Maximum Daylight

Allows exterior natural sunlight to flow throughout interior cubicles, reducing electricity costs.

Executive Speech Privacy

Acoustic perimeter seals ensure discussions in boardrooms and HR offices remain confidential.

Zero Downtime Fitting

Pre-cut off-site at Rahman workshop; assembled on-site in 1-2 days without messy plaster or paint.

Demountable & Reusable

If your business relocates to a larger facility, the entire partition system can be dismantled and reinstalled.

Frequently Asked Questions

Commercial & Corporate Glass FAQs

Common questions from homeowners, builders, and business owners in Sialkot.

Is tempered glass safe for high-traffic office spaces?

+

Yes, 10mm and 12mm tempered safety glass is 5 times stronger than standard float glass and engineered to withstand heavy impact. In the unlikely event of breakage, it crumbles into small, blunt granules preventing injury.

Can you customize frosting with our company logo?

+

Absolutely! We use computerized vinyl plotters to cut sharp corporate logos, frosted modesty bands, and modern geometric patterns onto privacy films.

Do glass partitions block sound adequately for conference meetings?

+

Yes, with acoustic silicone seals and drop-down acoustic door bottom thresholds, our 12mm tempered partitions deliver 38–42 dB sound attenuation, exceeding commercial conference room standards.
