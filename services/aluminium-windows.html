Frequently Asked Questions

Energy-Efficient Windows FAQs

Common questions from homeowners, builders, and business owners in Sialkot.

Which window style is better: Sliding or Casement?

+

Sliding windows are ideal for compact rooms as they do not occupy exterior or interior swing space. Casement (openable swing) windows provide 100% full-opening ventilation and slightly superior acoustic compression sealing. We offer both styles!

Does double-glazing fog up between the glass panels?

+

No. Our double-glazed units are vacuum-sealed with primary polyisobutylene and secondary structural silicone, packed with molecular sieve desiccant beads to absorb all internal moisture permanently.

Can we install security grills with aluminium windows?

+

Yes, we manufacture matching integrated aluminium safety grills and high-density insect fly screens that clip seamlessly into the window profile.
