WHY CHOOSE OUR DESIGNER & SMART MIRRORS

Rahman is Sialkot’s premier master craftsman for bespoke designer LED mirrors and smart touch vanity mirrors. Using 5mm ultra-clear Belgian float glass, we eliminate greenish tint and wave distortion, delivering 100% true-to-life HD reflections. Our mirrors feature high-density IP65 waterproof LED strips (50,000-hour rated lifespan), seamless capacitive touch switches with stepless dimming, and anti-fog heated demister pads for crystal-clear clarity even during steaming hot showers. From TikTok-viral organic wavy velvet floor mirrors to minimalist arched smart mirrors, each piece is handcrafted to your exact dimensions.

+

+

All our smart mirrors feature IP65-rated moisture-proof LED drivers, sealed touch sensor modules, and silicone-encapsulated wiring that safely isolate all electrical components.
