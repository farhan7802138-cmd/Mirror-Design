WHY CHOOSE OUR DESIGNER & SMART MIRRORS

Rahman is Sialkot’s premier master craftsman for bespoke designer LED mirrors and smart touch vanity mirrors. Using 5mm ultra-clear Belgian float glass, we eliminate greenish tint and wave distortion, delivering 100% true-to-life HD reflections. Frequently Asked Questions

Designer & Smart Mirrors FAQs

Common questions from homeowners, builders, and business owners in Sialkot.

How long do the LED lights last and can they be replaced?

+

Our commercial LED strips are rated for over 50,000 hours of continuous use (over 10 years of everyday use). All drivers and strip components are modular and can be easily serviced or replaced.

How does the mirror connect to power in the bathroom?

+

We provide an integrated CE-certified waterproof 12V transformer. It can be hardwired directly into a standard bathroom wall switch or plugged into a nearby wall socket.
