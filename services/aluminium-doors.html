Frequently Asked Questions

Architectural Aluminium Doors FAQs

Common questions from homeowners, builders, and business owners in Sialkot.

What is the standard price per square foot for aluminium doors in Sialkot?

-

Pricing generally ranges from Rs. 850 to Rs. 1,650 per square foot depending on the profile section gauge (1.2mm, 1.6mm, or 2.0mm heavy section), glass specification (single clear vs. double-glazed reflective), and lock hardware. We offer 100% free on-site measurement visits and provide an itemized quote.

How long does fabrication and installation take for custom aluminium doors?

+

Standard fabrication takes 5 to 7 working days once measurements and finishes are confirmed. On-site installation is completed cleanly in a single day with zero disruption to your household.

What warranty do you provide on aluminium doors and lock hardware?

+

We provide a 5-year warranty on architectural powder-coated profiles against peeling or fading, and a 1-year warranty on rollers, hinges, and euro-cylinder locking mechanisms.

Can aluminium doors look like authentic wood?

+

Yes. We specialize in Italian woodgrain sublimation coatings in Dark Walnut, Golden Oak, and Teak. You get the warmth of timber with the structural strength, fire safety, and termite immunity of aluminium.

Do you offer free laser measurement visits in Sialkot Cantt and Daska?

+

Yes, Master Rahman and our technical team provide complimentary on-site laser surveys throughout Sialkot City, Sialkot Cantt, Daska, Sambrial, Pasrur, and Wazirabad Road.
