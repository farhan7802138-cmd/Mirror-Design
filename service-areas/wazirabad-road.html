Architectural Fabrication Tailored to Wazirabad Road

Properties situated on Wazirabad Road face continuous heavy vehicular traffic, industrial particulate matter, and acoustic noise. Standard lightweight commercial aluminium sections quickly rattle and leak air. We deploy 1.6mm and 2.0mm heavy structural aluminium frames paired with laminated safety glass or double-glazed acoustic panels to deliver sound isolation, seismic stability, and maximum security for retail and corporate premises.

Our mobile laser measurement team regularly visits properties across Wazirabad-Sialkot Highway corridor, Small Industrial Estate access, export manufacturing belt, commercial plazas, ensuring accurate millimeter-level sizing before our factory begins extrusion cutting, glass tempering, or electrical LED wiring.
