Our Heritage

Built on Precision, Driven by Integrity

Founded in Sialkot by Master Rahman, our journey began over a decade and a half ago with a clear objective: to bring genuine architectural-grade standards to a market inundated with flimsy, thin-gauge aluminium and distorting glass.

What started as a specialized workshop hand-crafting bevelled vanity mirrors and secure sliding windows has grown into Sialkot's benchmark for modern architectural fabrication. Today, we equip luxury residential estates, corporate headquarters, and retail showrooms across Sialkot Cantt, Model Town, Daska, Sambrial, and greater Punjab with custom structural aluminium and luminous smart mirrors.

Every single door, partition, or backlit LED mirror that leaves our workshop is inspected for millimeter-level accuracy, airtight rubber gasket seals, and flawless optical reflection.

Why We Stand Apart

THE 4 PILLARS OF RAHMAN QUALITY

We refuse shortcuts. Here are the core technical standards we guarantee on every residential and commercial installation.

Virgin 6063-T6 Alloy Only

We strictly reject recycled scrap aluminium. All framing utilizes prime virgin 6063-T6 structural alloy with 1.6mm to 2.0mm wall thickness that never sags, warps, or rusts.

Copper-Free HD Glass

Belgian-grade, silver-coated float glass that guarantees distortion-free optical clarity and total resistance to black edges and moisture discoloration in Pakistani bathrooms.

Laser-Accurate Site Measurement

Every home and commercial project begins with a 100% free on-site laser survey to compensate for out-of-plumb walls, ensuring an airtight, rattle-free final installation.

Clean Installation & Warranty

Factory-preassembled panels mean on-site installation is finished in a single day with zero mess, complete joint sealing guarantees, and long-term hardware support.

Proven Methodology

HOW WE DELIVER PERFECTION

From your initial consultation to the final hardware inspection, here is our transparent 4-step workflow.

01

Free Site Laser Audit

Our senior technician visits your site in Sialkot, takes laser-accurate measurements, and evaluates opening structures and lighting.

02

Custom Engineering & Quote

We review profile gauges, glass coatings, locking hardware, and smart sensor options, giving you a detailed itemized quote with zero surprises.

03

Precision Workshop Fabrication

In our dedicated Sialkot workshop, profiles are CNC-mitered, glass is polished and tempered, and smart LED circuitry is rigorously quality-checked.

04

Clean 1-Day Installation

Our uniformed technicians install your panels, anchor security locks, inject weather-resistant silicone, and hand over the completed work.
