10 Google Business Q&As (Frequently Asked Questions)

Q1: What types of custom mirrors do you manufacture in Sialkot?

A: We manufacture a comprehensive selection of custom mirrors, including organic wavy LED floor mirrors, smart touch bathroom mirrors, arched vanity dressing mirrors, capsule mirrors, hexagonal and beveled mirrors, Hollywood vanity bulb mirrors, and custom backlit decorative mirrors. All mirrors can be tailored to your exact custom height, width, frame finish, and lighting preferences.

Q2: Do you provide free on-site measurements and estimates in Sialkot?

A: Yes, absolutely! We provide 100% free on-site laser measurements and detailed cost estimates across all areas of Sialkot, including Sialkot Cantt, Model Town, Daska Road, Sambrial, and surrounding vicinities. Simply contact us via phone or WhatsApp at +92 302 1054485 to schedule a convenient appointment.

Q3: What is the delivery and installation timeframe for custom mirrors and aluminium work?

A: Standard custom LED mirrors are typically manufactured, tested, and delivered within 4 to 7 working days. Comprehensive architectural aluminium doors, sliding windows, and office glass partitions generally take 7 to 14 working days, depending on project scale and glass tempering requirements.

Q4: Which areas do you serve outside of Sialkot city?

A: In addition to Sialkot city, our installation teams regularly operate in Daska, Sambrial, Pasrur, Jamke Cheema, Wazirabad Road, and nearby districts across Punjab. Free delivery is provided within Sialkot, with reasonable transit charges applicable for extended locations.

Q5: What gauge and quality of aluminium do you use for doors and windows?

A: We use only premium heavy-gauge prime aluminium extrusions (1.2mm, 1.6mm, and 2.0mm structural sections) from certified Pakistani and international extrusion mills. Our aluminium doors and windows feature durable powder-coated or anodized finishes that resist corrosion, fading, and peeling.

Q6: Are your bathroom mirrors moisture-proof and anti-fog?

A: Yes! Our bathroom LED mirrors feature IP65 waterproof LED lighting strips, sealed aluminum electrical enclosures, and optional integrated heated demister pads that keep the glass completely fog-free even during hot, steamy showers.

Q7: How do the smart touch sensors on your LED mirrors work?

A: Our mirrors utilize capacitive in-glass touch sensors. A quick tap turns the illumination on or off, a long touch activates smooth brightness dimming, and on dual-tone models, a tap allows you to switch between Warm White (3000K), Neutral Daylight (4000K), and Cool White (6500K).

Q8: What safety glass options do you provide for office partitions and shower cabins?

A: We use certified 8mm, 10mm, and 12mm thermal tempered safety glass for all shower cabins and commercial partitions. Tempered glass is up to 5 times stronger than standard float glass and breaks into small granular blunt pieces rather than jagged shards for maximum safety.

Q9: What warranty do you offer on your mirrors and aluminium installations?

A: We provide a 6-month warranty on our LED mirror lighting systems, touch sensors, and power supplies. Our aluminium fabrication and door structural frames carry a 1-year warranty, and glass installation alignment carries a 3-month warranty against manufacturing and workmanship defects.

Q10: What payment methods do you accept, and how do I place an order?

A: To place an order, contact us via WhatsApp at +92 302 1054485 or through our website form. After site measurements and quotation approval, a 50% advance deposit confirms your project. We accept Cash, JazzCash (+92 302 1054485), EasyPaisa (+92 302 1054485), and direct Bank Transfer.
